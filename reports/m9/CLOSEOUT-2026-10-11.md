# M9 — Living Governance Closeout

Date: 2026-10-11
Status: DONE

M9 is complete for the current GitHub program scope.

## Completed capabilities

- canonical GRS manifest/schema baseline;
- strict manifest-version validation;
- deterministic repository-surface auditing;
- repository-class-specific executable policy;
- fail-closed class-profile loading;
- class-taxonomy drift detection across schema, validator, profiles and pilots;
- immutable Action pinning and minimized checkout credentials;
- central GRS Control Plane regression coverage;
- pilot validation for profile, Haversine, Engineering Labs, Engineering Handbook and Career Intelligence;
- publication-boundary policy and validation;
- read-only publication-history path auditing;
- reusable fail-closed Autopilot health signal;
- protection against cross-workflow failure masking;
- documented GRS operating model;
- machine-enforced default-deny Autopilot merge-safety policy.

## Boundary

M9 completion does not imply that private repositories are ready for publication. Publication extraction, history safety, owner visibility approvals and domain-specific product gates remain in M4 and the relevant domain roadmaps.

## Ongoing mode

Living Governance now moves from milestone build-out to maintenance mode:

- regressions are caught by the control plane;
- taxonomy drift is rejected;
- merge-safety weakening is rejected;
- new controls are added only for demonstrated safety/correctness gaps or explicit portfolio requirements.

No perpetual governance expansion is required for M9 to remain complete.
