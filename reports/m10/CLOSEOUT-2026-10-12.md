## Completion meaning

M10 DONE means the GitHub Autopilot operating model is established and safe enough for steady-state use.

It does **not** mean:

- every future repository is maintenance-free;
- every connector will always be available;
- Governance v1 is complete;
- product-domain workers lose their authority;
- owner gates disappear.

Future Autopilot changes must be maintenance, verified defect repair, or explicit owner-approved evolution. They must not reopen M10 merely to create activity.

## Stop-building rule

Do not add more Autopilot infrastructure unless a repeated real failure proves that the existing model cannot handle it safely and cheaply.
