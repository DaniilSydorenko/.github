## M3 completion rule

M3 is complete because each planned Wave 1 flagship lane now has one of:

- merged public governance/remediation evidence; or
- merged central readiness/public-safety evidence with explicit ownership and publication gates.

M3 completion does **not** mean:

- all private repositories become public;
- Career Intelligence canonical data becomes public;
- Engineering Labs or Handbook are released;
- Haversine product modernization is complete;
- Ladvero is release-ready.

Those belong to later milestone/domain-specific gates.
